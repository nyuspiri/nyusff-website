2026 Honorable Mentions

Exceptional films recognized by our jury for their outstanding storytelling and contribution to the world of sports cinema at the 6th Annual NYU Sports Film Festival.

Honorable Mentions

The Bridge (1:18:14)

Directed by: Jimmy Hedger  |  Country: United Kingdom

The Bridge explores the untold story of Chelsea FC's global fanbase, capturing the rituals, sacrifices, and deep-rooted passion that unite over 100 million supporters across generations. From London to Nigeria, the film reveals how fandom transcends sport, becoming a shared identity and way of life.

The Soul of Football (30:00)

Directed by: Luchino Martino Simcik, Tom Pryce  |  Country: United Kingdom

Following six neurodiverse athletes from around the world, this documentary chronicles their journey to the Genuine Cup, an international tournament for players with intellectual disabilities. Through their stories, the film highlights football as a powerful tool for inclusion, community, and global connection.

Fighting Demons (29:47)

Directed by: Simon Stock  |  Country: United Kingdom

In the brutal world of bare-knuckle boxing, four fighters confront not only their opponents but their own inner struggles — ranging from disability and lost purpose to identity and legacy — revealing the deeply personal motivations behind stepping into the ring.

Last Shot (1:48:00)

Directed by: Andy Palmer  |  Country: United States
